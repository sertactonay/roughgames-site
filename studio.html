Rough Games is an independent studio from Türkiye. We make the games we cannot stop thinking about, and we would rather take the long road than release something we do not love.

Games that push back.

A good game asks something of you. It puts a wall in your way, hands you the tools to get over it, and steps aside. The win is yours because nobody gave it to you. That is the feeling we build for, in any genre and on any screen.

How we work.

Obsession first
Every game here starts as an idea that would not leave us alone. If that pull is missing, we do not start.
Hard, never unfair
Difficulty is a promise. When you lose you know why, and you want another go.
Finished means finished
A game leaves the building when it is ready, and we keep working on it after it has left.
